# Waveguide propagation

A guided calculation describes the transverse field through one or more modes.
Begin with the effective-area description, then choose a guide family. Each
family's practical chapter links to its equations, spatial resolution choices
and limitations. Coupling at a wall or along a taper is treated separately from
the choice of modes. The [spatial overview](../../introduction/spatial-description.md)
introduces these choices; the [waveguide gallery](../../auto_examples/waveguides/index.rst)
shows them in full simulations.
