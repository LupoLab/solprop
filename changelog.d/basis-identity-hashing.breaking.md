A modal run records its planes' basis identities in a third of the time it took
(0.1 s instead of 0.3 s before the first step of a 201-plane capillary with a pressure
gradient). The identities are formed differently, so a plane read from a file written
by an earlier version no longer matches a port's basis exactly and is refused as an
exact copy; rerun the earlier stage to relaunch its planes.
