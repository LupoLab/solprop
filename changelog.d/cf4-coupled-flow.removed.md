Removed the CF4 coupled-flow sections (ADR-077): `CoherentWall`'s `method`, `growth_limit` and `fallback` keywords, `solprop.translate.coupled.cf4_flow`, `cf4_defect`, `cf4_growth_bound`, `CF4_NODES`, `CF4_WEIGHTS` and `CF4_GROWTH_LIMIT`, the `"cf4"` section method, and the `flow.cf4` entry of a coherent wall's run record. They ran on `numpy_ref` alone. A varying coherent wall is integrated by the frozen-residual split, as by default; to shorten its advisory step, divide the sections (`CoherentWall(sections=...)`) or cap the step.
