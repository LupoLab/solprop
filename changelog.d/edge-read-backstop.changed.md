The transverse edge's read spacing has a growing backstop: `min(B, 4g/v_q)`, with `B` starting at `L/16` and doubling at each read whose reach has not grown by a tenth since it started, up to `L/4`. A free-space run whose spectrum holds, far from its edge, no longer takes extra steps for its reads: on the suite's `line` case the reads cost about 0.8 % against 24 % before, and 13 reads against 23 ([#396](https://github.com/LupoLab/solprop/issues/396)). On scaled versions of SEP 40's linear witnesses (414 packets that must stop) and on a self-focusing line beam in narrowed boxes (80 configurations), every run that stopped or warned under the former spacing still does.
