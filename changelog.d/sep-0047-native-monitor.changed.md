The native execution session (SEP 47's prototype, selected by tests and the benchmark harness) now runs a run's temporal boundary monitor itself: its launch judgement, cadence, quiet collar, split-collar cap, warnings, stops, plane rows, finish and resumed record are native, with the same decisions and record as solprop's monitor. A boundary warning reaches the caller as a `ServiceRequired` warning barrier, which the run's service loop emits; under warning filters that raise it, the run fails at that endpoint with its field available for the checkpoint ([SEP 47](docs/dev/seps/sep-0047-run-ownership.md)). A free-space run's transverse edge now runs on the native session too, no longer refused: its launch judgement, read spacing, reads, a requested collar's current and return, warnings, stops, record and resumed record are native, with the same decisions and record as solprop's edge on the same readings, and a point that raises several warnings serves each as its own barrier. The safety ramp's share in its band at the launch and its removal over each read interval are summed natively in another order, within a rounding bound of solprop's values that accumulates across reads, so their warnings and stops may differ from solprop's for a share within that bound of a threshold.
