Documented the limits of `FixedSchedule.from_history`. When the in-stage collar refuses a replayed schedule, the message now names the two remedies that replay a run's own history exactly: `absorber_scheme="split_step"` in both runs, or `dz_max` just under the printed limit in the discovery run. The docstring and the [fixed schedules guide](docs/guide/numerics/fixed-schedules.md) state the collar, interpolated-plane and non-abutting-history limits, relate `from_history` to `discover`, and replace the claim that a `Multistep` replay makes its adaptive run's truncation errors: compare two replays of one schedule instead (#82).
