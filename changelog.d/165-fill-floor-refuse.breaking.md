`Mixture.fill`, `Mixture.air` and `Mixture.with_fill` refuse `floor=` beside a constant pressure or a supplied `ZProfile`, and `with_fill` refuses it without a new `pressure=`, with a `MaterialError`; the simulation constructors and `prop_capillary`/`prop_waveguide` refuse `pressure_floor=` in the same cases. 0.1.0 accepted the floor there and ignored it. Drop the keyword where the pressure is constant or a profile; it applies only to end pressures or a stage table (#165).
