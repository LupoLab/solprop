`solprop.optics.Material` refuses a `temperature` for a glass or a mixture with `MaterialError`, where 0.1.0 ignored it: drop the argument there. The `temperature` of `Material`, `ModeCollection.slab` and `ModeCollection.vincetti` now defaults to `None`, which a built-in gas reads as room temperature (`solprop.constants.ROOM_TEMPERATURE`, 293.15 K) as before; given a `mixture=`, `slab` and `vincetti` now take the mixture's own temperature, where 0.1.0 refused the mixture beside its room-temperature default ([#158](https://github.com/LupoLab/solprop/issues/158)).
