The native backend evaluates and finishes its statistics natively from a plan lowered once (SEP 47 section 6.4): the half-maximum widths, the species reduction, the multi-channel electron density and peak ionisation rate, the plasma integration ratios, the profile statistics (`density`, `pressure`, `core_radius`) and a general modal basis's flux statistics (`omega0`, `physical_energy`, `spectral_flux`, `analytic_flux`, `fwhm_t`, `fwhm_t_outer`) no longer return to Python, and a general modal plane's statistics no longer copy the field to the host. Their z-dependent inputs are read from the problem's own tables; a profile statistic reads its profile lowered to a Chebyshev table whose recorded error bounds the change (about 2e-14 relative for a pressure gradient), and a modal power weight that varies in z is summed in a different order, a rounding-level change. The native execution session now takes every periodic-capable statistic as a native periodic row.
