Building a simulation no longer makes the caller's arrays read-only or keeps them by reference. Affected inputs: an absorber's rate arrays, the arrays passed to the translation layer's tables, `TabulatedDispersion`'s `omega` and `beta`, the backends' step histories and statistic rows, and the `save_planes`, knots and dispersion coefficients that the stepper and simulation settings, `PathVariation`, `TaylorDispersion`, `InterpolatedDispersion` and `ModalSampling` used to keep as views. Tables that the translation builds itself, and memoised ionisation rate tables, are still frozen in place, so this costs no extra copies (#759).
