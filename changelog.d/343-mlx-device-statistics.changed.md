On `mlx`, a saved plane's field-grid plasma statistics (the ionised fractions and peak rates at every node) are now evaluated on the GPU with the propagation's own rate, scan and population kernels, as the envelope grid's already were, rather than on the host in binary64 from every node's field. On a 128 x 128 Cartesian argon filament the statistics of a plane took about 3 s and now take under 0.1 s; the values agree with the reference to about 1e-6 of their largest. (#343)
