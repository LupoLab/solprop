A run no longer computes or records its planes' basis identities before its first step ([#874](https://github.com/LupoLab/solprop/issues/874)): a guide that varies along z paid a mode evaluation at every saved plane, about 0.3 s for 201 planes of a gas-filled capillary with a pressure gradient. The output schema is now 4: the plane-reference record (`RunMetadata.reference`) has no `basis` entry, and files of schema 3 are refused; read them with the solprop version that wrote them. A plane's basis is derived when it is read (`PlaneBasis.of_run`): a spatial or general modal plane's from the file, an ordinary mode set's from its modes at the plane's own position, so a plane saved at a natural step has one too. Those modes come from `field_plane(..., source=simulation)` or from the recipe the output records; a plane of a run from a script read without `source=` has no basis identity (`plane.basis.identity` is `None`), and `same_basis` refuses to copy it or apply a declared `BasisChange` to it, naming `source=` as the remedy. `PlaneBasis.identity` may be `None`, and `Recipe.from_output` also takes an opened output.
