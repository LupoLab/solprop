A resume continues only on an exact configuration hash, as in 0.1.0, and a refusal names the fields that changed (ADR-066). A changed tolerance or integrator, an optmat release, a commit that adds or moves a recorded number, and another kind of machine are all refused: launch a new segment from a saved plane, or write a new file. A long job has to stay on one CPU family to resume, since the record holds numbers computed on the machine that built it.
