Single and mixed precision are retired: solprop computes in IEEE 754 binary64 only ([ADR-077](docs/dev/decisions.md)). The `precision` keyword of `SimulationSettings`, the `Simulation.from_*` constructors and the `prop_*` functions, and a recipe's `precision` key, are gone; so are `solprop.translate.precision`, the `precision` field of `PropagationProblem` and the `precision` argument of `assemble`, the reference's binary32 path (`NumpySingleBackend`), `SINGLE_PRECISION_NOISE`, `solprop.driver.defaults.rtol_in_precision`, the `precision` argument of `default_rtol`, `default_controller`, `default_integrator`, `resolve_noise` and `solprop.backends.registry.available`, the run summary's precision row, the `precision` field of `solprop_native.BuildInfo`, and the guide's single-precision page and gallery example. The kernel's `run_options` reply lists the installed backends under `backends`, in place of `double` and `single`. Output written in single precision still opens.
