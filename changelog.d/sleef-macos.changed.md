On macOS arm64 the native backend's CPU kernels now take SLEEF's 1-ulp AdvSIMD vector `exp`, `log` and `sincos` by default (`SOLPROP_NATIVE_VECTOR_MATH=AUTO`), as x86-64 builds do: on an Apple M5 Max the comb-to-continuum case ran 0.84 of its time at one thread, and the tracking cases 0.93 on geometric mean, with none slower beyond its spread (ADR-072, #339). Linux aarch64 keeps the system libm until measured.
