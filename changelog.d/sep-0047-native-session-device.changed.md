Runs on a GPU build (CUDA or HIP) of the native backend now go through the native execution session, as on a CPU build, with the same steps, statistics and fields as before ([SEP 47](docs/dev/seps/sep-0047-run-ownership.md)): Adams and the embedded Runge-Kutta tableaux, adaptive or replayed, the low-storage replay, dense output (`interpolate_planes`), the modal, general and spatial routes (radial, Cartesian, line and planar, with their statistics and fluence widths), saved planes, periodic statistics rows, step planes, the temporal boundary monitor and a free-space run's transverse edge with a requested collar's current, the input and modified noise models and resumed Adams history. A `Batch` scan's group of native points runs in the native group session on a GPU build too. A saved plane's field crosses from the device once, into its destination, with no extra host copy. On a GPU build a coupled flow wider than 16 channels or off its eigenbasis route still uses the backend's own march on solprop's host engine, with a `HostMarchWarning`, as before.
