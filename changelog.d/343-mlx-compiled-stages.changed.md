The `mlx` backend compiles each stage on the GPU with `mx.compile`, so a stage's element-wise passes run as a few fused kernels and its graph is not rebuilt in Python, and forms a stage's propagator and its inverse in one kernel. A trial takes 1.5 to 2.6 times less time on the capillary, radial and Cartesian routes, measured on an Apple M5 Max, and the results are bitwise unchanged. `SOLPROP_MLX_FUSED=0` evaluates operation by operation.
