`VincettiMode`'s effective core radius uses the coefficient Rosa, Melli and Vincetti (2021) print in their Eq. 10, `3 + 20 wavelength / core_radius`, instead of 10, so its index, dispersion and fitted area change while the API and the loss do not. For the tutorial fibre (15 µm core, six 9 µm tubes with 180 nm walls) at 800 nm the effective radius grows by 0.52 % (16.85 to 16.93 µm), `n_eff` rises by 1.7e-6, `beta_2` moves from -479 to -471 fs²/m and the fitted area grows by 0.9 %. Filled with argon, its zero-dispersion wavelength moves 1.7 to 3.7 nm longer between 5 and 30 bar (619.6 to 622.3 nm at 10 bar). solprop now agrees with Luna's and pyJCMwave's coefficient, which was already 20. The coefficient is not a parameter: results with the old value of 10 need solprop 0.2 (#160).
