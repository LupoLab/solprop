A `ModeCollection` of step-index channels of one fibre, whose outer radii differ, now integrates `orthonormality()` and its mode matrices over the widest channel's disc instead of refusing the set it accepted, and channels built with `StepIndexMode.from_numerical_aperture` for one fibre are recognised as one guide; the new `StepIndexMode.same_guide` says whether two channels share a guide. `modes.collection.overlap` is now the pair's entry of `orthonormality`, integrated to the same 1e-12 and refusing non-convergence with `ModeError`, where it used `dblquad` at its default 1.5e-8 tolerance and only warned.
