A device's low-storage step costs less beyond its transforms. A free-space envelope's split-step temporal collar now transforms the state's own rows in place and holds no coarse buffers, a whole state at a time on a device unless `node_budget` is stated (106 chunks of 5 kernels become one block at 768 x 768 x 600); a flow's lossless bin takes no exponential; and a device's first input crosses to the scratch through tiles. On an NVIDIA A40 that step falls from 2.66 s to 2.56 s, with the fields unchanged to rounding. `Transform` has a new field, `chunk_budgeted`.
