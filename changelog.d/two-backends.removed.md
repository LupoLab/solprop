solprop has exactly two backends, `native` and `numpy_ref`, with no tiers and no plugin backends ([ADR-077](docs/dev/decisions.md)). `solprop.backends.registry.BACKENDS`, `BackendEntry`, `REMOVED_BACKENDS` and `FAR_FIELD_BACKENDS` are removed; `BACKEND_NAMES` lists the two. A backend name that is neither, `numba_cpu` and `mlx` included, now raises `BackendError` naming both wherever it is requested, where `select` raised `ValueError` for an unknown one; output and snapshots that record a retired backend still load. A recipe grid's retired `transforms` key is refused as an unknown keyword.
