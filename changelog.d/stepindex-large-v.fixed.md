`StepIndexMode` builds fibres up to V = 5000 and refuses larger ones with a message that says so; it refused most fibres above V of about 26, a 100 µm core of numerical aperture 0.22 at 1030 nm among them, because its root scan lost close pairs of HE and EH roots, and from a cladding argument of about 700 the unscaled modified Bessel functions underflowed. The lost roots are now bracketed by each family's own factor of the characteristic product, and every modified Bessel ratio and exterior field uses the exponentially scaled functions; effective indices below V = 26 move by at most one rounding (#148).
