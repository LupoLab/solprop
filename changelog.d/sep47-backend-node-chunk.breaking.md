The node chunk is the backend's execution choice, planned when the backend is prepared, not part of the translated problem (SEP 47, #361): `Transform.node_chunk` is gone, and `assemble`, `multimode_transform`, `free_transform`, `planar_transform` and `radial_transform` no longer take `node_budget` (`free_transform` no longer takes the time grid, nor `radial_transform` `n_pol`). `SimulationSettings.node_budget` keeps its meaning and its chunks, is now an execution setting that may change after translation and stays outside the resume key, and reaches the backend through `ExecutionOptions(node_budget=...)` (`select(..., options=...)` or the backend's `options=`); an explicit budget is authoritative. A budget too small for one node is refused by the backend with a `BackendError`, no longer by the translation. The default budgets and the rule are in `solprop.backends.node_chunk` (`DEFAULT_NODE_BUDGET`, `DEFAULT_SPATIAL_NODE_BUDGET`, `plan_node_chunk`), and every run records its `node_chunk`, `node_budget` and `node_chunk_source` in its backend metadata.
