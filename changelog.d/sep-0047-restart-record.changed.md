Each execution record now states what a restart keeps and restores, under `outcome.execution["restart"]`: whether a plane's or checkpoint's method history was `retained`, `omitted` or is `not_applicable`, with a reason (`explicit_policy`, `automatic_policy`, `automatic_size_limit`, `snapshot_required`, `unavailable_history`, `incompatible_representation`, ...), and in a resumed execution what it `restored`, `reset` (a Runge–Kutta resume's proposal and norm memory, an Adams history set aside) or found `not_applicable` (low-storage), and that the boundary monitor was reconstructed from its persisted records. A resume now warns once (`SolpropWarning`) when an Adams history it expected was omitted by the automatic 256 MiB limit or cannot be read back; `resume_history=False` is recorded without a warning. Files written before the record read as unknown, and resume as before ([SEP 47](docs/dev/seps/sep-0047-run-ownership.md)).
