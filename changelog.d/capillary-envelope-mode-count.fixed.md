`scalar_paraxial` coupling into a capillary counts HE1m profiles, not polarisation channels, against its benchmark envelope, so a two-polarisation port of four HE1m modes is no longer counted as eight. The capillary envelope (now `capillary-he1m-scalar-vs-leaky-marcatili-2026-11`) is measured up to 24 HE1m modes instead of 8, with its bound raised from 6e-3 to 6.2e-3 to cover the slightly larger discrepancy there.
