On a CUDA build, a Kerr-only free-space envelope's stage holds its transverse scratch in the state's order, so its rank-3 transform keeps frequency contiguous and its input and fold no longer cross layouts. A step on an NVIDIA H200 takes 204 ms at 768 x 768 x 600 where it took 225 ms, and 36 ms at 384 x 384 x 600 where it took 51 ms, with 1.3 GiB less memory there. Results are unchanged to rounding.
