`precision="single"` now admits the Adams integrator, `Multistep()` in its exponential formulation, on `numpy_ref` and on `mlx`, where its history stays on the GPU and each trial's flow, moments and compensation are formed there in double-float arithmetic. Binary32 Adams agrees with binary64 Adams to the method's own truncation on eight survival cases (the guide's single-precision page has the record), and on the capillaries it is closer to the binary64 result than binary32 Runge–Kutta is. A single-precision Adams history now resumes in binary32. The single-precision default integrator is still `RungeKutta()`. (#342)
