A device's low-storage step costs less beyond its transforms. On a CUDA or HIP build, a free-space envelope's split-step temporal collar transforms every row of the state at once, in place, rather than a node chunk at a time, whatever `node_budget` says, and holds no coarse buffers; a flow's lossless bin takes no exponential; and a device's first input crosses to the scratch through tiles. At 768 x 768 x 600 a step's collar takes 14 kernels and 10 transforms where it took 119 and 220. On an NVIDIA A40 the step fell from 2.66 s to about 2.56 s, and on an AMD Instinct MI300X a point takes 1.6 % less time. Results are unchanged to rounding ([#361](https://github.com/LupoLab/solprop/issues/361)).
